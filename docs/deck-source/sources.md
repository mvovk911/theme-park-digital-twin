# Research sources

Reviewed in the research conversation on 15 October 2026. These sources support technology capabilities. The theme-park architecture, pilot plan, illustrations and value hypotheses are original proposals, not claims that NVIDIA supplies a ready-made theme-park solution.

1. **NVIDIA Factory Operations (FOX)** — coordination of operational systems and specialist agents; operational twins. The article links to an availability notification page, so availability must be checked before implementation.
   - https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/factory-operations-fox-blueprint-ai-brain/
   - https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/nvidia-factory-operations-blueprint-notify-me/
2. **OpenUSD introduction** — scene composition, referencing, layering and time-varying properties.
   - https://openusd.org/release/intro.html
3. **NVIDIA Omniverse** — rendering and delivery of interactive experiences. The streaming overview points new deployments toward Omniverse Streaming on NVCF.
   - https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/ovas/latest/index.html
   - https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/materials-and-rendering/latest/rtx-renderer_rt_overview.html
   - https://docs.omniverse.nvidia.com/kit/docs/kit-manual/110.1.4/guide/app_streaming.html
4. **FIWARE for Digital Twins** — context brokers and NGSI-LD integration.
   - https://www.fiware.org/wp-content/uploads/FF_PositionPaper_FIWARE4DigitalTwins.pdf
   - Alternative device-state framework: https://eclipse.dev/ditto/intro-overview.html
5. **OPC Foundation: OPC UA** — industrial information exchange and information modeling.
   - https://opcfoundation.org/about/opc-technologies/opc-ua/
6. **IBM Maximo integration framework** — business-object REST integration.
   - https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/masv-and-l/maximo-manage/cd?topic=applications-integration-framework-overview
7. **ONVIF Profile M** — analytics metadata and events.
   - https://www.onvif.org/profiles/profile-m/
8. **AnyLogic Pedestrian Library** — modeling pedestrian density, service points and queues, including amusement-park applications.
   - https://anylogic.help/library-reference-guides/pedestrian-library/index.html
